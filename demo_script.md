# GlobeTrotter — 3-Minute Live Hackathon Demo Script

This script walks through the complete end-to-end user story optimized for a winning live hackathon presentation.

---

## ⏱️ Step 1: Introduction & Travel DNA Dashboard (0:00 – 0:30)

1. Open `http://127.0.0.1:8069`.
2. Highlight the **Modern Travel SaaS UI** (Deep Indigo `#4F46E5` palette, Glassmorphism, Warm Coral `#F97316` accents).
3. Click **"Demo Traveler"** on the fast login banner to instantly log in as Rohan Sharma.
4. Show the **Personalized Dashboard**:
   - **Travel DNA Profile Card**: 7-factor radar dimensions (`Culture 85%`, `Sightseeing 80%`, `Food 70%`) and traveler persona badge (`Cultural Connoisseur`).
   - **KPI Summary Cards**: Active Itineraries, Total Tracked Budgets, Next Destination, and Community Contributions.
   - **Recent Trips & Trending Destinations**.

---

## ⏱️ Step 2: Universal Global Search (`Ctrl+K`) (0:30 – 0:45)

1. Press `Ctrl+K` (or click the search trigger in the header).
2. Type `"Jaipur"`.
3. Showcase the instant omni-search results grouped into categories:
   - **Destinations**: Jaipur, India
   - **Curated Activities**: Amber Palace Sunset Tour, Hawa Mahal Photo Walk
   - **Accommodations**: Pearl Palace Heritage Boutique
   - **Community Stories**: 48 Hours of Royal Heritage in Jaipur
4. Click on a result to demonstrate deep modal navigation, then close the search modal.

---

## ⏱️ Step 3: Community Hub & 1-Click Itinerary Import (0:45 – 1:15)

1. Click **"Community Hub"** in the top navigation.
2. Highlight real traveler stories, verified traveler ratings, and travel style tags (`Culture`, `Adventure`).
3. Click the **Heart (Like)** and **Bookmark (Save)** reaction buttons on a story.
4. Click **"1-Click Import"** on a featured community post:
   - Select destination trip: *"Rajasthan Explorer"*.
   - Select target day: *Day 2*.
   - Review selected activity highlights with pre-computed estimated costs.
   - Click **"Confirm & Import"** — activities and expenses are instantly cloned into the active trip!

---

## ⏱️ Step 4: Multi-City Route, Stays & Health Diagnostics (1:15 – 1:55)

1. Open **"Rajasthan Explorer"** in the Itinerary Builder.
2. Show the **Multi-City Route Sequence**: Delhi → Jaipur → Udaipur.
3. Show the **Trip Health & Diagnostics Card**:
   - Dynamic **0–100 Health Score** with color-coded gauge (e.g. `92/100 · Optimal Itinerary Health`).
   - Factor bars: Budget Discipline (/30), Activity Load (/25), Pacing (/20), Stays Coverage (/15), Completeness (/10).
4. Show the **"Destination Accommodations & Stays"** section:
   - Click **"+ Find Recommended Hotels"** for Jaipur.
   - Show **0–100 Recommendation Match Score**, dynamic badges (`🏆 Best Overall`, `💰 Best Budget`), and **"Why this hotel matches your plan"** explanations.
   - Book a stay: Automatically creates an accommodation expense in the budget ledger with zero duplicate entries.

---

## ⏱️ Step 5: Day Sections & Smart Itinerary Balancing (1:55 – 2:25)

1. Scroll to the Day Schedule.
2. Show the **7 Section Types** with distinct iconography and styling:
   - 🎯 Activity / Sight
   - 🚆 Transit / Transport
   - 🏨 Hotel / Stay Check-in
   - 🍽️ Food & Dining
   - 🎭 Events
   - ☕ Free Time / Leisure
3. Point out the **Smart Itinerary Balancing Alert**:
   - Detects when a day is overloaded (>8 hours of activities).
   - Proposes a balanced schedule distribution.
   - Click **"⚡ Accept Suggestion"** — instantly rebalances activities across available days in 1 click!

---

## ⏱️ Step 6: Calendar, Timeline & Presentation Mode (2:25 – 2:40)

1. Click **"Calendar View"** — shows a clean grid with scheduled events, booked hotels, and daily cost totals.
2. Click **"Timeline View"** — shows connected journey nodes from Day 1 to Day 7.
3. Click **"Presentation Mode"** — full-screen executive summary with cover banner, hotels, and route cards suitable for stakeholder presentations.

---

## ⏱️ Step 7: Public Sharing, Cloning & Admin Intelligence (2:40 – 3:00)

1. Click **"Share Publicly"** — generates unguessable secure token `/shared/<token>`.
2. Open public share URL in new tab — demonstrates read-only snapshot with 1-click **"📋 Copy Trip to My Account"**.
3. Log in as **"Admin Demo"** and navigate to **"Admin Analytics"**:
   - Real-time platform KPI metrics (Travelers, Itineraries, Average Budget, Community Stories).
   - Top Destinations leaderboard & Travel Style distribution.
   - **User Management Table**: Live view of registered travelers, roles, preferences, and trip counts.
4. Conclude by highlighting the **Odoo 17 Model Architecture & PostgreSQL 17 Foundation**.
